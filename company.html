Est. London, 2021

Building finance worth having

Worthwhile started with a simple frustration: business banking hadn't kept pace with the businesses using it.

Founded in 2021 by a team of ex-bankers and engineers, we set out to build the financial platform we always wanted — one that moves money as fast as the internet, prices FX fairly, and treats a company's cash as something that should work, not sit idle.

Today, from our home in Canary Wharf, we serve more than 12,000 businesses across 180 countries — and we're only getting started.
