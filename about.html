The Atelier

Éclat began with a single idea: that true luxury should be felt, not announced.

Founded in London and made in the small ateliers of Italy, France and Scotland, Éclat is a study in restraint. We design a wardrobe of quiet essentials — coats that last a decade, cashmere that softens with time, eveningwear cut for a lifetime of occasions.

We produce in limited runs, never in excess. What we make, we make properly.

Our Craft

The hand of the maker

Every seam is closed by people we know by name. Our tailors have spent decades perfecting a single skill — the roll of a lapel, the set of a sleeve — and it shows in the way our clothes hold their shape, season after season.

— Made by hand, in Europe
