The Atelier

Made slowly,
made to last

Every Éclat piece begins as a single toile, cut and re-cut by hand until the line is exactly right. We work in small European workshops with fabrics milled in Italy and Scotland — never rushed, never in excess.

It is luxury that whispers: the weight of a coat, the fall of silk, the quiet confidence of something made properly.

— The House of Éclat
